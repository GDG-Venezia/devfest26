Getting there

Campus Scientifico Università Ca' Foscari
Via Torino, 155 Mestre, Venezia VE, 30170
Open directions
Closest station
Venezia Porto Marghera, 15 min on foot

About 1 km from the campus. It's a small stop between Mestre and Venezia Santa Lucia and only some regional trains call there, so check that yours does.

Main station
Venezia Mestre, 30 min on foot or a short bus ride

About 2.2 km away and served by every train. Urban buses go from the station to the "Torino Università" stop in front of the campus.

By bus
ACTV buses to "Torino Università"

Check routes, times and tickets in the AVM Venezia app: Saturday timetables can differ from weekdays.

By car
Close to the Mestre ring road

Via Torino is a few minutes from the Tangenziale di Mestre. Parking near the campus is limited, so arrive early or consider public transport.
