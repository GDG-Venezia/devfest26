Alessandro D’Angelo is a dedicated Mobile Developer at NTT DATA Italy, with a lifelong passion for technology, finance, and accessibility. His commitment to making applications accessible to all, while balancing development costs, is a cornerstone of his professional ethos. Alessandro’s enthusiasm for sharing knowledge led him to speak at Flutter Heroes 2024 in Italy, where he delivered a compelling presentation titled “The Importance of Accessibility - How to Build Better Inclusive Apps.”

At NTT Data, Alessandro has played a pivotal role in developing applications for major Italian companies, leveraging his expertise to optimize the implementation of webviews in Flutter apps. His career is marked by a holistic approach to mobile development, focusing not only on coding but also on creating the best possible user experience. This user-centric approach has been a key focus since his graduation.
