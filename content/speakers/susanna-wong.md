Susanna is a Staff Software Engineer passionate about all web technologies, and engineering excellence in the adoption of AI.

She has been a software developer with experience spanning across multiple industries, from small agile teams in big corporates ( CNN, Toyota Connected, FairFX, McKinsey/QB) to early stage startups, with extensive experience in web development (React, Ember, Angular, PWAs), GraphQL, and all things GenAI.

Susanna is currently a Staff engineer at Typeform, working at the realms of building products in cutting edge AI.

She is a Google Developer Expert in web technologies, and has spoken at multiple conferences worldwide, including Code Mesh 2017, Full Stack Fest 2018,Full Stack Europe 2019 and React Miami 2022. She currently sits on the Google Developer Advisory Board
