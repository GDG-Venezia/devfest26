Tarik is an self-taught developer who went from Basic on Desktop through Java for Android to Javascript for the Web and ended up as a huge PWA enthusiast. As such he is using Firebase and the latest web tech to solve all problems that come in front of him during his work at a logistics company. He believes that open source is the future of development so he made all his company libraries open source. Through his work as organiser of GDG Berchtesgadener Land and External Lecturer at Salzburg - University of Applied Sciences he tries to teach more people about modern web technologies.
